## Design reference
The current layout (grid header, project globe, tile gallery, drop-down contact panel) was
informed by screenshots of labs.griflan.com supplied by the site owner; earlier details (Work-page
card deck, blend-mode cursor, footer) by codeofsagar.in. **No code, copy, logos, mascots, 3D
models, textures or other assets from either site are included.** The keyboard, avatar styling, palette, copy and every component are original to this project.
